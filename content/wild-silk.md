## Searching a generative landscape

Wild Silk is part of a collection of seeded generative line experiments. Its emphasis is visual exploration through controlled parameters, repeatable seeds, and sequences of related forms.

A seed gives a particular composition an identity: something that can be revisited, compared, and refined rather than lost in a series of random trials.

## Parameters as choreography

A static setting gives one image. A smooth trajectory through parameter space gives an animation.

This suggests a few useful working principles:

- Hold a seed fixed while studying the effect of continuous parameters.
- Make interesting configurations easy to compare and recover.
- Prefer smooth transitions where a loop is intended to repeat.
- Treat density, framing, and visual rhythm as composition variables.

A technically continuous trajectory is not necessarily an aesthetically coherent one. That is part of the experiment.

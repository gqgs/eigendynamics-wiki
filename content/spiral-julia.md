## Complex dynamics as visual material

The quadratic Julia family illustrates how complicated structure can arise from a compact iterative rule. In standard notation, the iteration is **z(n+1) = z(n)² + c**, with complex z and a selected parameter c.

Small changes to the parameter can produce very different structures. This makes complex dynamics useful both for mathematical study and for visual exploration.

## Spiral Julia

This is an exploration of spiral-like composition, fractal geometry, and motion. One parameter explored during the work was **c = 1 − 0.38i**.

The intention is not merely to render a textbook diagram. It is to use mathematical behavior as material for an image with its own framing, rhythm, and visual identity.

The illustration on this page is a **schematic spiral**, not an original exported Julia frame.
